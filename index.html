The ask we will hear

Everyone will want MyScribe to do more.

Once MyScribe is live, the requests will arrive quickly. Help me prepare for my appointment. Remind me it is on Tuesday. Keep track of my medication. Book the follow up.

Every one of these is reasonable. Together, they would slowly turn MyScribe into another health app with a long menu, more screens, more safety work and more things for a tired person to learn. We would be competing with everyone, on everyone else’s ground.

Our answer is to do one thing really well, then take it everywhere.

MyScribe is a scribe. It listens when someone asks it to, writes down every word, and helps that person go back to what was said. That is rare, it is trusted, and it is exactly what people need.

Listening that reaches everyone

Reach is also about who we can hear.

A scribe in every corner is only as good as how faithfully it hears the person in front of it. The ideal listening models give us the greatest reach.

  • Every accent and dialect, from Portland to Glasgow to Lagos
  • Many languages, and conversations that move between them
  • Softly spoken people, and people whose speech is affected by illness or age
  • Busy rooms: a ward, a café, a kitchen table with the radio on
  • Three or more voices: a patient, a carer and a clinician, each written down as themselves
  • Patchy signal and older phones, so reach includes the people with the least

This is also our strongest story for commissioners. A scribe that hears everyone is an answer to health inequalities, and that is what ICBs pay for. Wherever a model is unsure, the words are highlighted, so the record stays honest.

The listening layer

The right ears for every country.

No single listening model hears the whole world best. So MyScribe sits on a listening layer, and we choose which model hears each country, each language, even each kind of room.

MyScribe might run on Speechmatics in the UK and Deepgram in India. Each choice is a setting we switch on, backed by evidence, with everything the person sees staying exactly the same.

How MyScribe is built in layers

The person

Asks first, records, reads the summary, asks the conversation. The same everywhere.

↓
Listening

The best model for this country and language writes down every word.

↓
Understanding

The summary and Ask Your Conversation, built from those words alone. Swappable too.

How we choose

  • Faithfulness to that country’s accents, languages and the way people really talk
  • Where the words are heard and held, matched to that country’s law and what its people expect
  • Several voices told apart clearly, in real rooms
  • Everyday and medical words written down correctly
  • Cost per minute that keeps MyScribe pennies, never pounds
  • The same promise from every supplier: nothing kept, nothing used for training

Switched on with a setting. Signed off with evidence.

Every model in every country is tested on consented test recordings before it goes live, compared against the one it would replace, and recorded in the safety case. People’s own conversations are never used to test or train anything.

No single supplier holds the keys to MyScribe. A buyer gets a scribe that can enter a new country on whichever model hears it best.
How reach pays

Free to place. Paid to use.

Putting Scribe it anywhere costs nothing. Every minute of listening is paid for, by the person or by someone on their behalf.

People pay as they go. Pennies per minute, topped up in the app, exactly as at launch.

Organisations fund their people. A charity or council that places Scribe it can load minutes behind its own code, so everyone arriving through them starts with credit.

Commissioners license populations. An ICB funds MyScribe for a whole area. Scribe it is how that population actually reaches it.

Gifted Minutes flows through it. Anyone can fund minutes for people who need them, and Scribe it puts those minutes within reach.

Every placement is a relationship, and every relationship is a lead for a paid one.
Reach and return

Scribe it brings people in. Trusted people brings them back.

Appointments come and go, so MyScribe has to earn a place in someone’s life between them. Reach solves the first conversation. Trusted people, the natural next step for share, solves the next one: a daughter reading Dad’s summary, a carer asking a question of last week’s visit, a family returning together.

The two work as a pair. Every placement brings someone new, and every trusted person they share with is another person who knows MyScribe before their own conversation that matters.

What reach asks of engineering

The team behind it after launch.

Reaching further changes the shape of the engineering work. Fewer new screens, more partners, more places, more voices. Whoever builds the next chapter will be doing six things well.

One owner

A single engineer who knows MyScribe end to end and stays with it.

Partner ready

Charity, council and platform conversations, held in UK hours alongside us.

UK where possible

Listening and storage in the UK, for example Deepgram through Azure UK, as Accuro already runs.

Safety at every door

Each new place Scribe it lives gets its safety case answer, side by side with the CSO.

A listening layer

Listening models chosen and switched per country and language by a setting, each one tested before it goes live.

Gentle costs

Running costs that stay predictable as reach grows, inside the agreed cap.

What travels with it

The same promises, wherever it goes.

  • The memory belongs to the person
  • MyScribe listens only when asked
  • Audio is deleted once written down
  • Data is never sold, never used to train AI
  • Unclear words are always flagged
  • Ask Your Conversation returns the exact words
  • Summaries leave out medicine names and doses
